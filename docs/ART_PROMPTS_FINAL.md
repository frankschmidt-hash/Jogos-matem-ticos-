# Prompts finais de arte — Prompt 07

A implementação usa SVG/CSS original e leve para não depender de serviços externos em runtime. Estes prompts documentam a direção caso assets rasterizados sejam produzidos futuramente.

## Família visual do portal
"Educational game portal for students ages 10–13, modern colorful vector illustration, geometric mathematics motifs, clean rounded shapes, energetic but not childish, high legibility, soft depth, no brand logos, transparent background, consistent navy outlines, violet/teal/coral/yellow palette."

## Banco Imobiliário Matemático — Cidade Prisma
"Original mathematical city-board game visual, isometric colorful districts, stylized property cards, translucent prism currency, tactile dice and pawn tokens, modern school-game aesthetic, clear iconography, no resemblance to Monopoly trademarks, vector style, accessible contrast."

## Corrida Maluca
"Original arcade math racing game, six distinct compact futuristic cars, bright track checkpoints, speed trails and a whimsical mathematical bomb icon, bold vector outlines, energetic particles, readable HUD style, no copyrighted vehicle designs."

## Corrida Numérica
"Original number racing game, sleek distinct educational vehicles, luminous numeric road, portal checkpoints with + × = symbols, combo energy meter, modern vector game UI, high contrast, no real-world car brands."

## Futebol Matemático
"Original stylized penalty stadium for an educational math game, abstract crowd, modern goal and net, friendly goalkeeper and kicker silhouettes, dramatic but clean stadium lighting, readable penalty scoreboard, vector illustration, no club or league branding."

## Restrições
- sem logos, personagens ou marcas existentes;
- evitar excesso de detalhes pequenos;
- manter leitura em 360 px de largura;
- transparência em ícones;
- contraste suficiente;
- não depender apenas de cor para estados.
